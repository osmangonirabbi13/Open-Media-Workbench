# ADR-0011: Open-Source Licensing and Dependency Supply Chain Policy

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Date:** 2026-10-13  
**Authors:** OpenTake Core Architecture Team  
**Deciders:** Lead Systems Architect, Open-Source Governance Lead  
**Target Release:** v0.1.0 Beta  

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement
OpenTake must establish its formal project source-code license and dependency supply chain rules. The license must preserve community access, prevent proprietary enclosure, harmonize with GPL FFmpeg sidecars, and protect contributors via explicit patent grants.

## 2. Decision Drivers
- Permanent protection against converting OpenTake into a closed-source subscription.
- 100% legal harmony with bundled GPL FFmpeg binaries.
- Explicit patent protection (FSF GPLv3 Section 11).
- Dependency selection discipline (YAGNI, license allowlist, committed lockfiles).

## 3. Options Considered
- **Option 1 (GNU General Public License v3.0 or later — GPL-3.0-or-later):** Strong copyleft; native alignment with GPL FFmpeg; explicit patent grants.
- **Option 2 (Apache License 2.0):** Permissive with patent grant; one-way compatible with GPLv3, but permits proprietary forks.
- **Option 3 (MIT License):** Highly permissive, but silent on patents and allows proprietary monetization of forks.

## 4. Proposed Decision
Adopt **Option 1 (GNU General Public License v3.0 or later — GPL-3.0-or-later)** as the recommended project license.

## 5. Consequences & Tradeoffs
- **Positive:** Guarantees OpenTake remains free and open source forever (following Blender, OBS Studio, and Audacity). Eliminates licensing friction when packaging GPL FFmpeg sidecars. Provides strong patent defense.
- **Negative:** Commercial entities cannot re-brand OpenTake into a closed proprietary product.
- **Tradeoff Accepted:** Aligning with creator community trust is a foundational product principle.

## 6. Risk Assessment & Alternatives Rejected
- Option 2 and Option 3 were rejected because permissive licenses do not protect the core codebase against closed commercial forks.
